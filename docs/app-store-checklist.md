# App Store Checklist

Before submitting SweepAlert to the App Store:

- Confirm the production Auth0 application and callback/logout URLs.
- Confirm the production Convex deployment and environment variables.
- Configure APNs production credentials.
- Verify Associated Domains for the invite domain.
- Verify notification permission copy and throttling behavior.
- Test first install, login, crew creation, invite acceptance, car move, widget install, and push delivery.
- Add privacy policy and support URLs in App Store Connect.
- Complete App Privacy disclosures.
- Upload a TestFlight build and test on a physical device.
